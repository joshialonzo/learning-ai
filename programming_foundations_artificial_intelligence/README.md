# Programming Foundations: Artificial Intelligence

## Exploring the fundamentals of AI

### Uncover AI's past

* AI's concept dates back to ancient myths, but modern AI began in the 20th century, with the term "artificial intelligence" coined in 1956 at Dartmouth College by John MCCarthy.
* Early AI focused on problem-solving and symbolic methods; the Turing Test remains a key benchmark for AI capabilities.
* AI evolved through practical applications in the 70s, neural networks in the 80s and 90s, and big data-driven AI from the 2000s onward, becoming a major driver of innovation across industries.
