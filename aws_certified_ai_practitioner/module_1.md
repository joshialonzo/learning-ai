## Introduction

This certification validates a candidate's ability to:

* Understand AI, ML, and Generative AI concepts, methods, and strategies in general and on AWS.
* Understand the appropiate use of AI/ML and generative AI technologies to ask relevant questions within the candidate's organization.
* Determine the correct types of AI/ML technologies to apply to specific use cases.
* Use AI, ML, and Generative AI technologies responsibly.

## Target Candidate Description

The target candidate description for the AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam:

* It Expects you to have up to six months of exposure to AI/ML technologies on AWS, which can include watching training videos or reading documentation—not necessarily hands-on experience.
* You should be familiar with core AWS services like EC2, S3, Lambda, and SageMaker, understand the shared responsibility model for security, AWS IAM for access control, and the AWS global infrastructure (regions, availability zones).
* Additionally, you need to know service pricing models and be aware of tasks that are out of scope, such as development, data engineering, hyperparameter tuning, and building full pipelines.

This knowledge helps ensure you can effectively use AI/ML technologies on AWS.
